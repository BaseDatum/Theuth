**Calls made**

- **Cap per export**: the brief says "the cap per export"; C3 has one cap per contact (`Contact.cap`, `ContactPatch.cap`) and none on `Export`. The picker is per contact and shows that contact's cap select; each export row carries its first-contact answer and Paused.
- **First-contact behaviour in settings** uses `contact_first_answer(contact, export, answer)` with no task pending (no patch field exists): assumed to just write the answer.
- **Grant / ungrant**: picking an agent or chat reuses its export when one exists, else `a2a_export_put({name: slug, target, description: title, paused: null})`, then `contact_update({exports})`. Taking the last grant of an export back also removes it (`a2a_export_remove`); Stop sharing removes the export and every grant (§12.10).
- **Export names**: `[a-z0-9_-]`, from the title or agent, ≤ 32 chars, `-2`… on a clash; `description` carries the readable title the chips show.
- **Agent exports**: `<agent file name>@<mesh self_name>` per agent with a desk (the alias `roster` writes), plus `theuth`.
- **Nearby `eid`**: `contact_pair({nearby, eid})` sends the TXT hint (`NearbyAgent` has no full id); the engine fetches and verifies the card. An unverified card is not greyed (`verified` stays false until that fetch); the engine's refusal shows in the ceremony's failed state.
- **Vendor**: `NearbyAgent` has no vendor field: no `eid` = another vendor, labelled `A2A · HTTPS` and its service type.
- **Ceremony detail**: `PairCeremony` has no machine field; the line shows `os_name · fingerprint(peer)`.
- **Mismatch** is this side's "The codes differ" (answers false, nothing stored); `rejected` covers the other side's Cancel or mismatch.
- **One window**: the ceremony renders inside the Add contact dialog (the window must stay open for it), so no second dialog stacks.
- **Inbox row**: plain text after `<contact_inbox/>` and its notice stays on the card as the contact's words (if E3 joins them into that row).
- **`</contact_message>` inside the words**: the last close ends the row, so a contact cannot close the card early and forge a wrapper after it.
- **Composer chip** `read only · bob's turn` (§12.10) not built: not in the brief's list, and no event says a contact's turn is running.
- **Phone/iPad**: §12.8 hides Contacts on a device; Network already shows only "Meshes this device can reach" there, `ContactAccess` and the ⋯ share items are gated on `hasLocal()`. "Phone (430 px)" is the node's own UI at that width.
  Lifted 2026-11-17: phones and iPads share through the connected engine (capability-gated): the ⋯ items need `shareRequirements` (`chat/requirements.ts`), Settings › Contacts and Access show on a device, only Add contact stays behind `hasLocal()`.
- **`RenameInput`** moved from `Network.tsx` to `settings/shared.tsx` (label is now a prop) so Contacts reuses it.
