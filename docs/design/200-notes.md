### Deviations from the design (and the brief)
1. **`crates/a2a/src/lib.rs` is touched** (two lines, not E1a's `Ctx::pass`): the `PeerRoute` literal in the route commit gains `full_access: p.full_access`, and the test helper's `config::Peer` literal gains `full_access: false`. A struct literal must name every field, so the Contract's `PeerRoute.full_access` and `Peer.full_access` cannot land without them. The design's "200 does not touch `lib.rs`" holds for every function 200-E will edit.
2. **`full_access_eligibility` is a method on `Agents`** (defined in `access.rs`): both halves need the supervisor's store, policy and owner check, and D17's caller in `rpc_messaging.rs` reaches the supervisor anyway. `row_eligibility` is the free function the Contract names. An unreadable row reads not visible (`-> bool`, fail closed) rather than an error.
3. **`session_route` takes only the canonical UUID spelling** (36 characters, lowercase, hyphenated): `uuid` would also parse braced, URN, simple and uppercase forms, and D2 wants one spelling for a scope, a path and a conversation export. `PeerAddress::parse` uses it for the suffix, so `peer:maya/session:<not a uuid>` is now malformed (it parsed as a remote name and failed later in `peer_by_name`); the sender's `not a session id` text is 200-E's mapping.
4. **`ListedSession.title` is `Option<String>`** (a session without a title yet); `kind` and `state` are the existing `PeerKind` / `PeerState` (so `"native"`, `"running"` as in the Contract's JSON); the activity is `PeerActivity` (its `stalled_secs` stays optional).
5. **`parse_request`**: `limit` is required (1..=200); `after` is any UUID `uuid` parses; an empty `query` is no query; `kind` is a `DirectoryKind` in its snake-case wire name; `active_only` is `1` or `0` (anything else is a 400); unknown keys are ignored.
6. **`merge` keeps the first answer's row on a duplicate id** (members in the order asked; stable sort), and a member that answered exactly `limit` rows keeps the cursor even when nothing follows (the next page is empty with `null`), as D8's rule reads.
7. **`ContactPatch.full_access` answers `not available yet`** rather than being ignored: a client that sends it learns at once that nothing was written. The desktop `NO_PATCH` is unchanged (the generated `ContactPatch` field is optional); 200-UI adds `full_access: null` with the switch.
8. **Markers, not bodies, in 199's hot functions**: `full.rs` (`full_shape`, `full_return`) and `rpc_messaging.rs` `contact_file_notice` (199-E1b's) carry no edit; their 200-E hooks are listed in `access.rs`'s module doc. Every other hook is a one-line `// 200-E:` / `// 200-UI:` comment right above its function; only two bodies changed: the header strip in `server.rs` `dispatch` (one call beside the principal strip) and the `NOT_YET` refusal at the top of `network.rs` `network_send` (200's alone).
9. **`offers::settled` sets `SettledCards.full_access` already** (one line, 200's function per the design's Phases): it reads `Cached.full_access`, which nothing sets yet, so the result is always false. It saves a dead-code allowance on the new field.

### What landed
- **Eligibility (D5)**: `Store::full_access_row`, one recursive query over the replicated ancestry: the row exists and is no advisor, no session up the chain carries a `contact_inbox` marker or a `communication` other than NULL/1, and the chain reaches an `owner` root within the lineage bound. `access::row_eligibility` wraps it (unreadable = not visible). `Agents::full_access_eligibility(&mut self, s)` = the row half, `owner_strict == Here`, `mirror`, then `Agents::full_access_live`: `Policy::reachable_from_owner` (not hidden, `enabled`, root scope `Owner`) and no contact inbox on the session or its root this run; `sendable` = not observed and the tree not in `Agents::paused`. ACP sessions are sendable; archive is not checked.
- **Setting (D1)**: `contact_update` with `full_access: Some(b)` writes `full_access = b` on every record of the group (single-column `RecordWrite`s in the one `commit_records`); the staged `Peer` changes, so `same_grant` rotates the epoch. `Contact.full_access_from` is `full-access/v1` on the card that counts (`offers_eid`).
- **Card (D11)**: `contact_card` always carries `full-access/v1` `{"granted": <this reader's Peer.full_access>}` before signing. The refresh keeps `Cached.full_access` (`sessions::card_full_access`) and counts its change as a change. `offers::changed` also reports a full-access toggle, so the contact is notified and refetches. `push_offers` hands the agent offers and `full_access` only from verified (`fetched`) cards: no entry means "card unknown" (`access::CardSays`).
- **Addressing and admission (D2-D4)**: `dispatch` answers 404 to `~session` by name and to a bearer on a `session:` path. It rewrites a contact's `/agents/session:<uuid>/...` to the mounted `/agents/~session/...` and sets `x-theuth-session`, after stripping any inbound copy. `Target::Session` goes to `Handler::admit_session`: `full access is off` (D4 (2)), then `access::FullAccessRow`, the row half on the receiving machine (plain `not owner`, never forwarded), principal export `session:<uuid>`, `ask_first = false`. `SendMessage` then runs `owned` (191's strict owner resolve; `forward::carried` accepts a canonical `session:<uuid>` segment, never `~session`). On the owner, `admit_principal`'s session branch checks the live half (plain `not owner`), and `admit_send` refuses visible-not-sendable with `this session does not take messages`. `GetTask`/`CancelTask` need visible plus the task in the per-session scope. Streaming, `ListTasks` and `SubscribeToTask` on `~session` are `UNSUPPORTED_OPERATION`.
- **Listing (D7, D8)**: `reach::Route::Sessions` → `sessions::serve`: 503 before ready, 403 `full access is off`, 400 `bad request`. It asks the agent `access::FullAccessList` (rows, row half, owners, lineages and job counts off the mailbox; live half and activity on it), with the answer raced against the admitted epoch (401, no title after it). The asking side is `client::Sender::list_sessions` → `sessions::list`: every roster member at once, each within `LIST_DEADLINE`, each answer re-cut to the request (`member_page`) with titles cleaned, then `merge`. Members that did not answer (timeout, unreachable, any non-200 other than 403) go in `partial` by machine name. `Err("<contact> has not given you full access")` comes only when every answering member said 403 and none was missing.
- **discover_peers**: `ListPeers` plans on the mailbox (`PeerPlan`: the directory rows and `NetworkState::granting`, the contacts whose verified card grants us; none for an inbox root) and finishes off it. The walk runs when the query names one granting contact, or on a `full:` cursor the query still names (otherwise `unknown cursor`); it returns the bounded prefix on page 1, then exactly the merged page and `full:<contact>:<uuid>`. The default asks every granting contact for 8 rows and splices them under the contact's row. `arrange` keeps contact rows in their order and reports what it hid. The contact's title is `full access`, `full access · more: …` or the refusal, plus ` · did not answer: …`. `PeerPage.partial` entries are `<contact>/<machine>`; `DiscoverPeersTool` adds the description sentence and the `partial:` line on every page, and shows `engine=` on listed rows.
- **Return path (D10)**: in `full_shape`, a fresh `peer:<c>/session:<uuid>` checks the latest verified card (`does not support full access yet`, `has not given you full access…`). With an unknown card it leaves without a conversation and asks for the card; otherwise it opens the outbound conversation under `session:<uuid>`. An answer on an inbound `session:` conversation needs my grant (``peer `x`: full access is off; nothing was sent``). `full_return` uses `NetworkState::conversation_closed`: settled with no card granting closes a `session:` conversation, and shares keep `share_closed`. `access::map_refusal` maps owner refusals for the sender (`no such session`, `does not take messages`, `needs updating`, not granted); `peer:<c>/session:<x>` reads `not a session id` (`supervisor::resolve`).
- **199 D17**: `admit_notice`'s re-check is `share_live || Agents::full_access_notice` (a live full-access route whose contact record is the notice's, and the chat `visible`). A paused tree's notice is held by `input`→`route` the way 179 holds automatic input: no actor, no row, and a resend stays one pending notice.
- **Revocation**: no new code. Off rotates the epoch (in-flight admissions and listings end 401), the card says `granted: false` after the notice, my replies are refused at home; block and remove work as before.
