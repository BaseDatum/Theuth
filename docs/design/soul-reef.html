The reef remembers

The first four are instruments: they measure the bus as it happens. This one is a body. It accretes the present at a living surface and keeps every day underneath it — the soul as a biography you can read, not a pulse you watch.

How to read it

  • Living fringe — the last few minutes. Vivid, moving, always growing.
  • Growth bands — one per day. The colour is that day's mix of message types.
  • Fractures — error clusters. Dark cracks with a red edge.
  • Gaps — silent days, almost translucent.
  • The pulse — when a type fires, a vein lights down through every day that colour appears. Its depth is how far back that kind of event goes.

Why it is a different object

Cost is O(days), not O(events). The past is inert geometry; only the fringe is simulated. It can run for years without getting heavier.

It survives restarts. One append-only record per day under ~/.theuth/soul/, and the body is rebuilt from it — the soul has a home and a memory.

It is the only one you can read backwards. Not a pulse you watch, a biography you open.
