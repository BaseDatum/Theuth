Default policy: a needs-you item that has waited less than 2 minutes while you are typing is held; it is delivered when you switch apps, stop typing for 20 s, or when it is 2 minutes old — whichever comes first. Several held items arrive as one banner. Finished work never interrupts; it waits for the next breakpoint. Errors are immediate.

6 · Keyboard grammar

⌥⌥ (double-tap) or ⌃⇧SpaceOpen the Attention panel over whatever you are doing; the focused item is the oldest needs-you. If nothing needs you, the panel opens as the Ask bar instead.
1 2 3 … ⏎Answer the focused item; Enter takes the recommended option. The answer waits 3 s before it is sent — the receipt row counts down.
⎋During the 3 s: undo. Otherwise: close the panel, focus returns to the app you were in.
J K · ↓ ↑Move between items. Needs-you items first, oldest first; finished work after.
RReply in words (free text, or a deny-with-reason). Hold ⌃S to say it instead (96's voice key).
SSnooze 30 m (the agent is told you will come back; it keeps working on anything not blocked).
EClear a finished-work receipt. Clearing never cancels anything.
D · O · ⌘OOpen the diff · open the mini-board · open the session in the main window — the three steps of progressive disclosure.
⌘1…⌘9 in the Ask barRoute a request to a specific agent instead of letting theuth route it.
