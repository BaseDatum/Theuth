1 · The loop from glance to answer without leaving your app

1 · glanceGold on the rail or a count in the menu bar: something needs you. No gold, nothing does.
2 · ⌃⇧SpaceThe panel opens over your app, key but non-activating; the oldest needs-you card is focused.
3 · 1 / 2 / 3 · ⏎One keystroke picks an option. The receipt row counts down 3 s.
4 · ⎋ or wait⎋ takes it back. Otherwise the answer is sent; the item becomes a receipt; focus is already back in your app.
5 · laterFinished work arrives at your next breakpoint with two ready replies; E clears it.

8 · Keyboard grammar

⌃⇧Space (rebindable) · ⌥⌥ opt-inOpen the panel on the oldest needs-you item; if nothing needs you, open the Ask bar. Hold to dictate.
1 2 3 · ⏎Answer the focused card; Enter takes the recommended option. The answer waits 3 s (0–5 s in Settings).
⎋During the hold: take it back. Otherwise: close the panel; your app never lost focus.
J K · ↓ ↑Move between items — needs-you oldest first, then finished work.
RReply in words (also deny-with-a-note, send-back-with-a-note).
S · ESnooze 30 m · clear a finished receipt (never cancels anything).
D · O · ⌘OOpen the diff · the mini-board · the session — in the main theuth window (progressive disclosure).
⌘LJump to the Ask bar — whatever you type goes to theuth's desk, which routes it.
