200 CONTACT-FULL-ACCESS · the ultimate trust level for a contact

The ruled behaviour: any of a full-access contact's sessions can message any of yours and see your session list. Each side grants its own; private chats are never listed or reachable; nothing is synced. Names, titles and ids are fictional.
