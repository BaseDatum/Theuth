## 1. How a contact reaches a chat today

- **Address parsing has two layers.** `PeerAddress::parse` recognizes `session:<uuid>` separately from `peer:<string>`; the latter remains one opaque remote name initially. The entire remote name is limited to 64 characters, not separately bounded contact/export segments (`crates/agent/src/communication.rs:97-98,879-903`). `NetworkState::peer_by_name` then splits `<contact>/<export>`, accepts only a nonempty lowercase-ASCII/digit/`-`/`_` suffix, and matches the prefix only against a contact (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:1027-1043`).

- **Outbound routing is export-shaped.** The outbound message gets its export either from the full-conversation shape or from the qualified address suffix (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:4549-4566`). The iroh client turns it into `/agents/<export>/rpc`, or `/rpc` when absent (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:494-500`). Bare `/rpc` means the first export granted to this authenticated contact, resolved before forwarding; no grant means 404 (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:149-158`).

- **Authentication and the share gate precede dispatch.** `ContactCaller(eid)` selects a principal by authenticated endpoint; inbound copies of the internal principal header are stripped. An export must be in that principal’s granted set and currently open; the sole special exemption is contact-only `~return`. Authentication is checked before export existence (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:378-427`).

- **Export → session resolution is static today.** `Live::build` constructs chat targets from `staged.exports` and agent targets from `staged.agents` (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:244-253`). `Handler::admit` produces `A2aPrincipal { binding, export, session, route, contact, ask_first }`; chat exports contain the exact session, while agent exports resolve/create the contact’s dedicated inbox through `ContactInbox` (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:607-628,655-708`).

- **Every admitted contact share is full collaboration.** Admission constructs `ContactOrigin` with `ShareMode::Full` (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:657-665`). Full delivery opens a conversation, records provenance, then enters ordinary peer intake unscoped: it can steer a running turn or open one; its A2A task completes as a receipt when consumed, not with the agent’s eventual answer (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:1-8,368-405,410-450`).

- **First-contact hold remains per contact × export.** Explicit `Allow` bypasses it, explicit `Block` refuses, and missing/Ask requires a hold (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:666-675`). Admission parks the task separately from the session’s active slot/queue (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:1747-1768`); the hold becomes `AuthRequired` and expires after 24 hours (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:757-760,3725-3758`).

- **Fresh sharing already counts as consent.** `grant_patch` starts a newly granted export on Always Allow when no old answer exists; an existing Ask-first answer survives. It also enforces **one principal per exported session**, across other contacts and HTTP peers (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:2015-2051`). Full access should not be implemented by silently manufacturing ordinary grants for every session: that would inherit this exclusivity rule.

- **Allow once versus Always:** Always writes the standing grant answer; Allow once releases the current local hold without writing Always; Block removes/blocks the contact. Allow once on another machine’s hold is refused, while Always can be written there and acted on by the owner (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:2583-2643`; `docs/design/191-notes.md:143-149`).

- **191 ownership is strict on contact paths.** `SendMessage` calls `Handler::owned` after resolving the session and before `A2aSend`; `ContactOwned` reaches `contact_owned` (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:711-719,808-816`). It accepts only `Owner::Here`; known foreign ownership yields `not owner` plus internal owner detail, and unknown/unreadable ownership yields plain `not owner` (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:127-159`). `owner_strict` requires an existing readable row owned by this engine; an empty owner on a mesh engine is unknown, unlike lenient owner helpers (`crates/agent/src/store/contacts.rs:498-509`).

- **Owner forwarding preserves that boundary.** A2A `not owner` is `-32030`; its internal owner detail is consumed by the forwarding layer rather than exposed directly (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:104-113`; `crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:181-227`). Only `SendMessage`, `GetTask`, `CancelTask` are carried, only on accepted export/return RPC paths, with at most two resolutions (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:34-46,75-83`). `GetTask`/`CancelTask` can select the admitting engine from the task ID; sends resolve the target session instead (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:166-179`).

- **The owner independently authorizes the carried request.** `a2a_forward` requires `Forwarded(peer)` and checks the mesh messaging-off setting (`crates/a2a/src/rpc_hub.rs:17-30,40-59`). `Ctx::forwarded` checks readiness, its method/path allowlist, and that `caller` is still a contact here, then runs its own A2A router with `ContactCaller`. It does not forward again (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:330-386`).

- **Replies use a separate return grant.** `/agents/~return/rpc` is contact-only, never an advertised export; a conversation’s grant slides for seven days (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:25-29`). `full_return` verifies live contact/binding, known nonexpired outbound conversation, strict session ownership, and share continuity, then refreshes the thread (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:681-732`). New sends/conversation reuse currently depend on an advertised `ContactOffer` and its export name (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:267-309`).

- **Tool ceilings are not a contact sandbox in full mode.** Full chat messages enter normal unscoped peer intake (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:410-450`). Full agent inboxes use their agent ceiling: `run_full_inbox` calls `clear_contact_limits`, which removes contact allowlisting and installs only the supplied ceiling (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:516-529`; `crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:3590-3622`). The registry explicitly says full shares keep the agent’s tools/ceiling and normal LAN setting (`crates/tools/src/registry.rs:1076-1080`).

- **Full collaboration does not confer approval authority.** Contacts receive the A2A router and contact extension routes, not generic owner RPC dispatch (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:216-235,254-267`). `resolve_approval` is an owner-audience RPC resolving an `ApprovalId`, not an A2A method (`crates/tasks/src/rpc_hub.rs:14-23,40-49`). The model-facing peer frame also explicitly says a peer cannot approve or speak for the user (`crates/agent/src/frame.rs:64`). Normal owner-configured approvals remain normal; there is no new automatic-approval power implied by the trust level.

## 2. How `discover_peers` builds rows

- **Entry/source:** the tool asks `ListPeers` with caller session, query, cursor, limit, active-only and kind (`crates/agent/src/peers.rs:653-667`). `PeerRead::fetch` reads the durable `Store::list_sessions`, resolves missing lineages, reads engine names, and gets job counts (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:6973-7001`).

- **Own-mesh candidates are permission-filtered, not “all rows.”** `directory_from` mirrors lineages, determines the caller’s root, then excludes self and any candidate rejected by `Policy::route` (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:7014-7056`). That policy requires enabled endpoints and Owner scope on both roots for cross-root communication; advisors are unknown to routing (`crates/agent/src/communication.rs:1428-1448`). A contact inventory promising all non-private sessions therefore cannot blindly reuse this caller-relative filter.

- **Exact row shape:** `PeerInfo` contains `address`, `name`, `kind`, `relationship`, `state`, `send`, `reply`, `stream`, `activity`, optional `title`, `tags`, optional `engine`, optional `reachable` (`crates/agent/src/communication.rs:388-430`). This is the safe existing projection to reuse, rather than exposing `SessionSummary` wholesale.

- **Own-session values:** address is `session:<uuid>`; relationships distinguish parent/child/sibling/ancestor/descendant versus other root. Native/ACP/node kind comes from the row’s backend; foreign-owned rows are `Remote`, local states come from running/approval/wait/live/stored state (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:7058-7109,7114-7136`). Same-tree names may use their brief name; other roots use an explicit alias or short ID (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:7074-7083`).

- **Title/activity caveat:** title is filled only for the caller’s own tree, while tags cross roots. Foreign-owned rows have no activity here; engine names and connected-owner reachability are provided instead. Local activity is populated afterward (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:7110-7161`). “Same fields as own mesh discovery” does not currently mean live activity or full titles from every engine.

- **Contacts/export rows:** `remote_peers` adds A2A/Remote/Idle rows with send/reply true, stream false, no activity/engine. A contact gets `peer:<contact>` tagged `contact`; each offer gets `peer:<contact>/<export>`, its description as title, and contact/kind/state tags (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:878-938`). Contact inbox roots are deliberately prevented from seeing the contact list (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:880-906`).

- **How offers arrive:** the receiver’s contact-specific card is built from exports granted to that caller; grant answers become `allowed`, `unavailable`, or `ask_first` offers (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/offers.rs:134-165,289-313`). The caller fetches the card on the contact connection, verifies its signature against that endpoint, then reads offers, full capability and machine name (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/offers.rs:381-403`). `of_card` reads **skills**, validates/deduplicates names, caps count/title lengths and parses tags (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/offers.rs:168-214`). Notices trigger refresh rather than carrying a session inventory (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:300-332`).

- **Existing inventory-like mechanisms:** owner `list_sessions` returns the durable session list and decorates running/remote-running state (`crates/agent/src/rpc_sessions.rs:130-132`; `crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:8239-8265`). It is not a contact operation. A2A `ListTasks` lists admitted A2A tasks under the resolved principal, not native sessions (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:858-878`). No contact-session inventory operation exists in the inspected contact route table (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:254-267`).

## 3. Contact record, replication and settings

- **Durable record:** `ContactRow` has `id`, alias, display name, machine, binding, paired timestamp, pairing engine, blocked, removal tombstone and merge target. All non-key fields are optional because replication can deliver columns separately (`crates/agent/src/store/contacts.rs:40-59`). Endpoint anchors and per-export grants are separate rows; grants carry `granted` and `first_contact` (`crates/agent/src/store/contacts.rs:61-85`).

- **UI/RPC view:** `Contact` adds path/fingerprint, granted exports, first-contact answers, remote `shared` offers, `full_capable`, problem state, pairing machine and accepted contact machines (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:76-126`). `full_capable` is only the remote card’s existing full-collaboration advertisement—explicitly informational, never authorization (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:100-104`). There is no contact-wide full-access flag in these current structs.

- **Replicated tables:** `RECORD_TABLES` is `contacts`, `contact_endpoints`, `contact_grants`, `a2a_exports`, `contact_threads`, `contact_rosters`, `contact_roster_heads`, `contact_holds`. Address hints and accepted remote-roster local state are local (`crates/agent/src/store/contacts.rs:1-27`). `replicate_records` attaches these alongside projects/cost owners to the per-column record replica, outside the session capture log (`crates/agent/src/store.rs:985-999`).

- **Migration convention:** migration 0059 requires one primary key plus `hlc TEXT`, nullable unconstrained payload columns, no NOT NULL/UNIQUE/CHECK/FK constraints, and removal tombstones rather than deleting contact rows (`crates/agent/migrations/0059_mesh_contacts.sql:1-19`). Modern replica apply also has table parking/retry machinery; constraints still must not become a validity mechanism for contact data (`crates/replica/src/apply.rs:31-35,73-112`).

- **Adding a persisted trust field:** the concrete seams are a nullable column migration, `ContactRow`, its explicit SELECT, `RecordTable::Contacts.columns`, staging/effective-contact facts, then the outward `Contact`/`ContactPatch` contract. Writes should remain single-column `RecordWrite`s rather than rewriting a stale whole row (`crates/agent/src/store/contacts.rs:44-59,292-304,330-331,361-367,458-494`; `crates/a2a/src/config.rs:283-304`; `crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:79-126,199-212`). Recommendation: missing/malformed/not-yet-arrived must mean **not full access**.

- **Wire schema:** current `SESSIONS_WIRE_SCHEMA` is **67**. A contact-record-only column does **not** change it: its bump rule concerns session-log tables. It also supports a semantic JSON compatibility bump, as 67 demonstrates, so revisit if the implementation changes replicated session/message semantics instead (`crates/agent/src/store.rs:701-723`). The notes confirm record-replica-only 191 migrations did not bump it (`docs/design/191-notes.md:143-144`).

- **Mixed-version record surprise:** an old attached schema can skip an unknown column and record the batch. After upgrade/reattach, reannouncement under existing clocks is needed to recover it; there is a regression test specifically for that behavior (`crates/replica/src/tests.rs:723-750`). Missing trust data must fail closed during that interval.

- **Current settings surface:** `ContactPatch` supports rename, blocked, incremental grant/revoke; deprecated whole-list `exports` is refused (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:199-212,1972-1988`). The RPC forwards the typed patch to `ContactUpdate`; current update handling writes blocked/alias columns and commits records (`crates/a2a/src/rpc_messaging.rs:127-140`; `crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:2062-2110`). Existing per-contact authorization is blocked plus per-export grant/first-contact state—not a trust-level enum.

- **Revocation participation matters:** `Staged::same_grant` defines whether old authorization epochs survive a configuration change; `unchanged_exports` governs preservation across changes (`crates/a2a/src/config.rs:405-445`). A full-access field must participate in authorization-epoch invalidation, not merely appear in the UI/record.

## 4. Private chats: not a `sessions.private` flag

- **Creation marker:** `CreateSessionOptions.retention` is Durable or Private, with a client view required for a private root (`crates/agent/src/lifecycle.rs:18-35`). Private creation allocates `Store::open_memory`, creates its root there, and calls `open_private` (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:12784-12817`).

- **Where privacy lives:** supervisor `private` and `private_member` maps identify live private trees/members (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:334-341`). Each `PrivateRoot` owns its separate in-memory store, scratch resources, owner lease and membership (`crates/agent/src/lifecycle.rs:248-260`). `open_private` installs `Retention::Private` resources and the owner record (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:12419-12460`). The store itself has a private boolean; this is not a durable session-row flag (`crates/agent/src/store.rs:849-891`).

- **Listing/mesh exclusion is structural:** the durable list reads the durable store; private trees never enter its peer topology (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:1099-1101,8239-8256`). Own-peer discovery reads that same durable store (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:6981-6989`). Private stores have neither capture session nor log, and record replication explicitly refuses them (`crates/agent/src/store.rs:849-854,872-883,994-995`).

- **Search exclusion:** `SearchSessionPage` uses `self.store`, not a selected private tree’s store; the index reads searchable message bodies/titles from that store (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:9648-9663`; `crates/agent/src/search.rs:260-295`).

- **Direct-ID admission must preserve nonexistent behavior:** private authorization is owner-peer/tree-bound and unknown IDs never create (`crates/agent/src/lifecycle.rs:178-201`). A contact endpoint should resolve only durable eligible rows, not enumerate the supervisor’s live actors/maps or call a helper that falls back to creating/loading a session. The strict contact owner predicate already treats absent/unreadable rows as not owned (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:147-159`).

## 5. Serving a new operation and advertising capability

- **Contact control-plane seam:** `reach::Route::of` recognizes GET Agent Card, POST offers-changed Notice, and GET roster Path; all other requests go to `forward::route` (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:216-231,254-267`). `contact_route` applies the contact’s existing rate/concurrency admission before handling the operation (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:271-287`).

- **Recommended home for session listing:** a new contact-only extension route alongside Card/Notice/Path, with a typed bounded response and explicit full-access check. This avoids forcing a listing through an export or confusing it with A2A `ListTasks`. If expressed as a JSON-RPC extension instead, intercept it explicitly before the SDK/export router: today ordinary JSON-RPC forwarding only carries the three task/message methods and export-shaped paths (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:34-46,75-83,159-195`; `crates/a2a/src/server.rs:858-878`).

- **Existing extension advertisement/readers:** common card construction advertises `machine/v1` with machine/OS parameters and `full/v1` without parameters (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:551-589`). `card_machine` reads the former, `card_full` tests presence of the latter (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:701-728`). `members/v1` has its own URI and reader deserializing extension params (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/rosters.rs:17-21`; `crates/a2a/src/contacts/offers.rs:217-227`).

- **Per-contact capability/grant data can be personalized before signing.** `contact_card` identifies the caller, builds that caller’s offers, appends the roster extension after budgeting its bytes, then signs the finished card (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/offers.rs:289-337`). A new `full-access/v1` capability/reader belongs here; do not reinterpret `full/v1`, which already means implementation support for full per-share collaboration, not this new permission (`crates/a2a/src/contacts.rs:103-104,701-707`).

## 6. Addressing an arbitrary session across machines

- **Session IDs are UUIDs, generated UUIDv7**, not engine-local counters; canonical session addresses parse that UUID directly (`crates/runtime/src/lib.rs:431-440`; `crates/agent/src/communication.rs:894-896`). Ownership is separate metadata used to select the machine (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:7098-7100,7129-7135`).

- **Owner-side front door already exists.** `Forwarded` identifies an authenticated mesh forwarding engine on `theuth/rpc/1`; the mesh RPC server inserts both internal peer identity and this marker (`crates/agent/src/rpc_forward.rs:160-164,525-538`). Ordinary peer messages go to the owning engine as `peer_deliver`, retaining accepted/failed receipt semantics (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:6332-6352`).

- **Do not pass contact input off as an ordinary mesh sender.** `peer_deliver` is mesh-internal and requires `Forwarded`; its receiving supervisor verifies that the forwarder owns the envelope’s sender session, plus messaging/global communication checks (`crates/agent/src/rpc_turns.rs:304-325`; `crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:6231-6274`). A contact instead needs authenticated binding provenance and independently checked full-access authority at the destination, as existing `a2a_forward` does (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:342-369`).

- **Admission change required:** add an explicit session-target shape distinct from an export. Current HTTP parsing, grant membership check, static `Target`, principal `export`, outbound offer lookup, thread creation and forwarding allowlist all assume exports (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:382-427,607-628,701-708`; `crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:267-309`; `crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:75-83`). Preserve binding, route epoch, contact origin, strict owner resolution, receipts/dedup and return conversations while replacing export authorization for the direct-session case.

- **Address/UI consequences:** a new qualified contact-session address must survive the 64-character remote-name limit and suffix grammar; `peer:<contact>/session:<uuid>` does not currently pass the suffix validator (`crates/agent/src/communication.rs:97-98,898-900`; `crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:1027-1035`). Bare `session:<uuid>` already means the owner’s own mesh and must not silently change namespace.

## 8. Risks, surprises and explicit design decisions still needed

- **“All non-private” is broader than today’s discoverable peers.** Existing policy hides advisors and isolated/disabled routes, and `Store::list_sessions` hides advisors by default (`crates/agent/src/communication.rs:1428-1444`; `crates/agent/src/store.rs:3262-3284`). Decide whether “all” changes those independent routing rules; do not equate Owner scope with privacy.

- **Not every non-private session is messageable.** Session kinds include hidden Advisors and watch-only Observed children, which are explicitly never driven/messaged; Ask roots are otherwise chats (`crates/agent/src/lib.rs:2964-2987`). Peer rows mark observed children send/reply false (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:7120-7124`). Current contact admission also rejects sessions with an ACP `agent` backend (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:1475-1479`). Arbitrary native children versus ACP/node/observed children need an explicit contract.

- **Agent desks are ordinary durable roots with operational significance.** Roster creates them through default `CreateSessionOptions`, names them, then `MarkDesk`s them (`crates/roster/src/lib.rs:993-1027`). Direct full-access contact messages would address the real desk, unlike an agent export’s per-contact inbox (`crates/a2a/src/server.rs:677-688`).

- **Routine runs carry their own restrictions.** Schedule roots are unattended/isolated, and every fire reapplies approval pins and any tool cap (`crates/schedule/src/lib.rs:2123-2131,1528-1549`). Full access should not accidentally clear those caps or conflate isolated automation with private storage.

- **Meeting sessions retain their live toolset.** Meeting startup creates an agent session and installs the lean recording toolset (`crates/meeting/src/lib.rs:2595-2607,2669-2677`). Broad session reachability must not replace that toolset or treat peer text as a live-transcript/owner request.

- **Sandbox engines are in scope unless deliberately excluded.** Bring-up pairs an engine with the mesh; VM guest configuration is class `vm`, hub `never` (`crates/sandbox/src/bringup.rs:17-26,154-187,230-239`). Contact-family construction uses paired IDs plus self, with no class filter (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/members.rs:64-71`). “All machines” therefore deserves an explicit VM/container policy, not an assumption that it means only laptops/desktops.

- **Do not call `clear_contact_limits` indiscriminately for every reachable chat.** Besides changing caps, it marks the session as a contact inbox; inbox status suppresses contact-directory rows (`crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:3598-3603,880-906`). Full inbox policy restoration is a narrower operation than arbitrary-session admission.

- **Do not use `ContactScoped` as the full-access authority check.** It tests allowlist state on the session/ancestors, while full-share sessions intentionally lack those contact restrictions (`crates/tools/src/registry.rs:1058-1080`). The new grant needs its own authenticated contact authorization predicate.

- **Return-path assumptions need updating with direct targets.** `full_shape` checks inbound conversations against the contact’s granted-full export set; `full_return` checks card-derived share continuity. New thread creation keys by offer/export name (`crates/agent/src/communication/network/full.rs:239-246,288-302,720-732`). Simply admitting a direct send while leaving these unchanged can break later replies or revocation.

- **191 rosters establish accepted machines, not session inventory.** They carry signed membership chains, with per-machine accepted state; contacts are refused before the post-sync readiness latch/reconcile (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/rosters.rs:1-8`; `crates/agent/src/store/contacts.rs:1-7`; `crates/agent/src/communication/network.rs:339-347`). Listing through one member can use replicated durable metadata, but it cannot manufacture fresh activity from other owners (`crates/agent/src/supervisor.rs:6981-7001,7145-7160`).

- **Availability remains owner-directed.** The contact client tries admitted members, preferring the last answering/reachable member; `not owner` permits trying the next, but an uncertain outcome after sending is not replayed elsewhere (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/reach.rs:387-449`). Full-access listing must tolerate differing accepted rosters, replication lag and offline owners without silently granting a take, syncing transcripts, or turning a received contact stream into generic owner RPC access. Existing strict ownership and owner-side reauthorization are the reusable boundary (`crates/a2a/src/contacts/forward.rs:181-227,330-386`).
