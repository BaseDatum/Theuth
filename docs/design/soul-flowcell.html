Flow cell

Looking straight at the harness. The tube comes out of the port toward you, bends ninety degrees and runs across the face, then bends back into the second port. The legs hide behind the elbows — you see the glass run standing off the wall, and the events that rise out of the depth, cross, and sink back in.

what flows

One particle per event. Hue is hashed into 166–224° (teal → blue); alerts break the band in red and carry a ring.

depth

Particles rise out of the port behind the left elbow and sink into the right one. They ride a laminar profile — fast at the centre, slow at the wall — and swirl slowly around the axis, front and back of the bore.

the glimpse

You only ever see the crossing. Everything else happens inside the harness.
