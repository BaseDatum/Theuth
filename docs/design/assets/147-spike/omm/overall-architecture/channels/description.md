Where people and other agents reach the engine (79 files / 52,204 lines). `signal`, `slack`, `telegram` turn chat messages into session input and render replies per channel; `a2a` is the agent-to-agent boundary; `link` pairs phones and the relay (`relay` is the separate binary); `nodes` connects remote nodes over the link protocol. All three chat channels depend on `double`, `roster`, `tasks` and `artifact` to post files and route to desks.
