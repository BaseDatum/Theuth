Every crate is a plugin started by the `t` binary (`crates/theuth/src/main.rs`) on a shared `runtime::Runtime` with kameo actors; `crates/rpc` exposes them to the clients over one jsonrpsee WebSocket. This perspective groups the 49 crates into 11 layers chosen by what they depend on and who calls them; the Cargo path-dependency graph (334 edges) is the ground truth behind every arrow.
